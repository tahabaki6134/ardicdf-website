# Manufacturing navigation and content

The home page and /services now organize discovery by seven manufacturing routes. Products and sectors remain accessible as application examples in the existing archive. /compare (English) and /karsilastir (Turkish) compare two stable method IDs. The selected methods are passed to the existing enquiry form; no messages are sent by comparison interactions.

The shared catalog and page components are identical in both repositories. Only manufacturing-site.ts selects the edition. English and Turkish have reciprocal language alternates on the home, index, comparison and matching detail pages. Existing indexed service URLs remain available and point to the new guide. No new country domain is registered or claimed by this change. Additional editions should add reviewed translations and real domain configuration, with self-canonicals and reciprocal alternates only after publication. The Istanbul production location stays explicit.

Costs are qualitative and conditional on matching geometry, quantity, finished surface and service conditions. They are not price quotes or numerical engineering claims. Raw EPS is distinguished from coated/framed assemblies; carbon stiffness is distinguished from impact tolerance. FDM is distinguished from resin printing. Tooling for carbon parts is distinguished from tooling made of carbon. Autoclave, prepreg, certified load ratings and specific high-temperature capabilities are not promised.
